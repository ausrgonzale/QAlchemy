# Requirements Evaluation Deliverable

## Task

Evaluate the supplied Jira requirement for readiness for Development and QE.

## Evaluation

Follow the Agent Instructions and the Requirements Review Definition of Done standards when performing the evaluation.

The final evaluation must address:

- Requirement quality and readiness
- Acceptance criteria quality and readiness
- Development implementation readiness
- QE / test-case readiness
- Acceptance-criteria validation readiness
- Strengths, gaps, ambiguities, assumptions, and dependencies
- Supporting evidence and rationale
- Recommended actions
- Overall readiness determination

## Output

Produce:

- A structured requirements evaluation result.
- A completed requirements evaluation report.
- Supporting evidence used during the evaluation.

The completed report must clearly explain the findings, evidence, and rationale
for the readiness determination.

## Traceability

The evaluation must be traceable to:

- The supplied requirement.
- Its acceptance criteria.
- Evidence gathered during the evaluation.
- The structured evaluation result.
- The completed requirements evaluation report.
